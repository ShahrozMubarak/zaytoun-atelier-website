GOOD TOGETHER

Your bag.

A home for your wardrobe.

  1. Add your pieces. Use the Studio to add products. Pick a built-in shape or upload a transparent garment image and align it.
  2. Export your catalog. Download products.json. It includes your products, image placements, and brand palette.
  3. Publish on your website. Replace products.json in the supplied source folder and upload the folder to your host. Visitors load that shared catalog.
  4. Connect your store. The source includes catalog, cart, and try-on integration events. Connect these to Shopify, WooCommerce, or your own backend using INTEGRATION.md.

Studio edits are local to each browser until you export and publish them. The demo does not process payments or provide an authenticated admin backend.
